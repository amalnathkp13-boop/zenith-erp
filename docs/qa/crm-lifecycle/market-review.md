# What eight CRMs can teach IdeaERP

Reviewed 19 November 2026 against IdeaERP CRM Specification v1.0 and Zenith ERP master at `96622c4e`. This purposive comparison covers eight commercial and open source products. It measures documented capabilities and does not rank market share or guarantee feature availability in every edition. A shared theme is traceability. Sellers can see why a lead needs attention, managers can examine the deals behind a forecast, and an accepted deal can be traced to its ERP quotation. The review maps each practice to the ERP workflow and identifies the remaining acceptance work.

## Salesforce as a reference for the revenue lifecycle

Sales Cloud describes separate records for people, customer accounts and opportunities. Conversion relates those records so a seller can track several deals with the same customer. Sales Cloud also provides forecast categories for management rollups. IdeaERP already had lead and opportunity records. This change adds a deliberate match to an existing ERP customer and two forecast views in each deal currency. A probability-weighted estimate multiplies a deal’s value by its estimated chance of closing. The category view assigns the entire amount to the stage’s configured category. Open stages default to Pipeline until an administrator selects Best Case, Commit or Omitted. Closed Won always rolls up to Closed and Closed Lost to Omitted. The view shows each category independently. Forecast submissions, overrides, quotas and hierarchy rollups remain future work. [Sales Cloud objects](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/sales-cloud-configuration-basics/customize-sales-cloud) and [forecast categories and hierarchy](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/revenue-intelligence-setup/review-your-collaborative-forecasts-setup) and [single-category rollups](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/sell-smarter-with-pipeline-inspection/understand-pipeline-health-with-metrics-and-charts).

| Salesforce reference | IdeaERP foundation | Change in this branch | Additional scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sellers qualify products, assemble bundles and apply pricing from a shared catalog. [Catalog and qualification rules](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/revenue-cloud-design/set-up-your-product-offerings) | The native Sales quotation editor already uses products, quantities and line discounts and invokes ERP pricing and GST logic. | A seller can open that editor from a customer or opportunity and retain the relationship to the opportunity. | Guided bundle compatibility, eligibility rules, channel-specific price books and commercial approval policies require separate acceptance. |
| Contracts, purchased assets, order fulfillment, billing and metered usage extend the process beyond a signed quote. [Agentforce Revenue Management](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/agentforce-revenue-management-quick-look/get-to-know-agentforce-revenue-management) | ERP Sales, Accounting and Subscriptions already have their own orders, invoices and subscription screens. | CRM links into the native quotation workflow. Sales owns revisions and the resulting order. | Contract authoring/signature, asset-based amendments, renewal quoting, usage rating, fulfillment orchestration and revenue recognition have no demonstrated end-to-end CRM implementation in this change. |
| An ERP can own orders and return the definitive ID and status to Salesforce. Real-time calls, queued events and batch synchronization serve different latency and data-volume needs. [Salesforce Architects integration patterns](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/fundamentals/guide/integration-patterns) | IdeaERP CRM and Sales share party and order identifiers in Moqui. | The local transaction can link an opportunity to its ERP quotation. The workflow requires no external replication for these two modules. | A separate Salesforce deployment would need field ownership, external IDs, authenticated event or API exchange, duplicate/retry handling and reconciliation. No connector to a live Salesforce org is included. |

## ERP quotation verification

The disposable test submits the native CRM and Sales forms, reads the saved records in PostgreSQL, and extracts the three customer-facing PDFs. Separate sessions request the same quotation concurrently. The resulting draft and its revision stay linked to the opportunity. [Regression test](check-opportunity.py)

Both examples use two units at ₹100 and a 10% discount.

| Accepted GST | Taxable value | GST | Quotation | Sales order | Invoice payable |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0% | ₹180.00 | ₹0.00 | ₹180.00 | ₹180.00 | ₹180.00 |
| 18% | ₹180.00 | ₹32.40 | ₹212.40 | ₹212.40 | ₹212.40 |

Previously, the order header stored ₹200.00 before discount and tax, and the linked invoice rounded the payment down to ₹212.00. The native Sales total now includes the line discount and agreed tax. Conversion carries those saved amounts into the invoice, including paise. The test checks the printed amount, amount in words, payment balance and commission. It also covers a direct order priced from the catalog and an accepted quotation rate that differs from the catalog rate.
