# What eight CRMs can teach IdeaERP

Reviewed 19 November 2026 against IdeaERP CRM Specification v1.0 and Zenith ERP master at `96622c4e`. This purposive comparison covers eight commercial and open source products. It measures documented capabilities and does not rank market share or guarantee feature availability in every edition. A shared theme is traceability. Sellers can see why a lead needs attention, managers can examine the deals behind a forecast, and an accepted deal can be traced to its ERP quotation. The review maps each practice to the ERP workflow and identifies the remaining acceptance work.

## How other CRMs handle the work

| CRM | Documented behavior | Relevance to IdeaERP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salesforce | Stages feed forecast categories and managers can roll up opportunities by period and hierarchy. [Forecast setup](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/revenue-intelligence-setup/review-your-collaborative-forecasts-setup) | Show the stage, period, currencies and amounts that contributed to each estimate. |
| Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales | A prioritized work list combines due activities, customer history, manual follow-ups and sequences. [Seller work lists](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/sales/prioritize-sales-pipeline-through-work-list) | Give each pending follow-up a visible owner, due date, linked record and completion action. |
| Pipedrive | Administrators can set an inactivity threshold for each stage. Scheduling a future activity alone does not establish recent contact. [Deal inactivity](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/the-rotting-feature) | List open opportunities that need attention and explain how the inactivity date is calculated. |
| Freshsales | Contact and company attributes, positive or negative engagement and product events contribute to contact scoring. [Scoring signals](https://crmsupport.freshworks.com/support/solutions/articles/50000002580-what-is-predictive-contact-scoring-how-does-it-work-) | Label scores calculated by local rules as such. Preserve source and activity evidence. |
| HubSpot | An import can match by record ID or unique property, and contacts can match by email. Workflows distinguish a future event from records that already match a filter. [Duplicate handling](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/deduplication-of-records) and [enrollment rules](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/set-event-enrollment-triggers) | Reuse a scoped customer match during conversion and make automated actions safe to retry. |
| Zoho CRM | Blueprint lets admins make selected fields compulsory during particular transitions. [Blueprint transitions](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/process-management/blueprint/articles/design-a-blueprint) | Validate a lost reason at the same service boundary used by both board and detail views. |
| Odoo | An opportunity can open existing quotations or create a quotation using the Sales app's customer, product lines and terms. [Opportunity quotations](https://github.com/odoo/documentation/blob/19.0/content/applications/sales/crm/acquire_leads/send_quotes.rst) | Preserve a link to the ERP quotation ID and use its existing product, price and tax workflow. |
| Twenty | Custom objects and fields, table/board/calendar views, and workflows with manual, event, scheduled and webhook triggers share a record model. [Twenty feature guide](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-docs/getting-started/key-features.mdx) | Reuse the ERP entities and permissions when presenting different views of the same records. |

## Salesforce as a reference for the revenue lifecycle

Sales Cloud describes separate records for people, customer accounts and opportunities. Conversion relates those records so a seller can track several deals with the same customer. Sales Cloud also provides forecast categories for management rollups. IdeaERP already had lead and opportunity records. This change adds a deliberate match to an existing ERP customer and two forecast views in each deal currency. A probability-weighted estimate multiplies a deal’s value by its estimated chance of closing. The category view assigns the entire amount to the stage’s configured category. Open stages default to Pipeline until an administrator selects Best Case, Commit or Omitted. Closed Won always rolls up to Closed and Closed Lost to Omitted. The view shows each category independently. Forecast submissions, overrides, quotas and hierarchy rollups remain future work. [Sales Cloud objects](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/sales-cloud-configuration-basics/customize-sales-cloud) and [forecast categories and hierarchy](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/revenue-intelligence-setup/review-your-collaborative-forecasts-setup) and [single-category rollups](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/sell-smarter-with-pipeline-inspection/understand-pipeline-health-with-metrics-and-charts).

| Salesforce reference | IdeaERP foundation | Change in this branch | Additional scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sellers qualify products, assemble bundles and apply pricing from a shared catalog. [Catalog and qualification rules](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/revenue-cloud-design/set-up-your-product-offerings) | The native Sales quotation editor already uses products, quantities and line discounts and invokes ERP pricing and GST logic. | A seller can open that editor from a customer or opportunity and retain the relationship to the opportunity. | Guided bundle compatibility, eligibility rules, channel-specific price books and commercial approval policies require separate acceptance. |
| Contracts, purchased assets, order fulfillment, billing and metered usage extend the process beyond a signed quote. [Agentforce Revenue Management](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/agentforce-revenue-management-quick-look/get-to-know-agentforce-revenue-management) | ERP Sales, Accounting and Subscriptions already have their own orders, invoices and subscription screens. | CRM links into the native quotation workflow. Sales owns revisions and the resulting order. | Contract authoring/signature, asset-based amendments, renewal quoting, usage rating, fulfillment orchestration and revenue recognition have no demonstrated end-to-end CRM implementation in this change. |
| An ERP can own orders and return the definitive ID and status to Salesforce. Real-time calls, queued events and batch synchronization serve different latency and data-volume needs. [Salesforce Architects integration patterns](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/fundamentals/guide/integration-patterns) | IdeaERP CRM and Sales share party and order identifiers in Moqui. | The local transaction can link an opportunity to its ERP quotation. The workflow requires no external replication for these two modules. | A separate Salesforce deployment would need field ownership, external IDs, authenticated event or API exchange, duplicate/retry handling and reconciliation. No connector to a live Salesforce org is included. |

## ERP quotation verification

The disposable two-tenant test submits the CRM and Sales forms and reads their persisted PostgreSQL records. Two independent authenticated sessions submit the same quotation request concurrently. One draft is created. Its revision links back to the same opportunity, and conversion links the ERP order and invoice. The customer record opens that customer's quotation, order and invoice lists. Finalizing and settling the tax-free invoice takes its outstanding balance to zero. To reproduce, load the all-modules fixture, set `CRM_QA_PASSWORD` to its test password and run `python3 docs/qa/crm-lifecycle/check-opportunity.py <local-base-url> <postgres-container>`. [Regression test](check-opportunity.py)

Both fixtures quote two units at ₹100 each with a 10% discount. The invoice uses the tenant's configured tax engine and the product's tax classification when issuing the invoice.

| Test fixture | Taxable amount on quote and order line | GST on quote and order line | Native order header | ERP invoice total including GST |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Tax-free quote and tenant with default tax fallback | ₹180.00 | ₹0.00 | ₹200.00 | ₹180.00 |
| India GST engine, registered seller and 18% product slab | ₹180.00 | ₹32.40 | ₹200.00 | ₹212.40 |

The test creates both invoices and finalizes and settles the tax-free invoice. The taxed invoice is verified in draft.

**Existing Sales discrepancy.** The native quote and order screens read `OrderHeader.grandTotal`. Mantle derives it from item quantities and unit prices. Zenith's quotation line records its discount and GST in additional fields. In both fixtures the native header therefore displays ₹200.00 before either adjustment. The quote print template sums the line's taxable amount and GST, and the configured invoice independently calculates the same ₹212.40. Existing payment and commission logic consumes the native header. Reconciling that stored total across ERP consumers and legacy documents needs a separate Sales accounting change. The CRM handoff reuses the native records.
